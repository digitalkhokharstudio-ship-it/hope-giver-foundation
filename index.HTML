Privacy & Transparency Policy

1. Data Collection: Hope Giver Foundation adheres to rigorous data privacy standards. We only record communication details (Name, Email, Phone number) voluntarily provided for transaction tracking and donor acknowledgment.

2. Payment Integrity: Financial dealings occur via direct, encrypted banking lines or verified remittance services. We do not store sensitive credit card or personal banking credentials on this platform.

3. Media Protocols: All visual records published across our public transparency channels are captured with permission from community leaders to guarantee accountability while maintaining participant dignity.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the foundation geographically based?

Our headquarters and primary operations are centered in Lahore, Pakistan, extending aid throughout peripheral rural areas and brick kiln settlements.

How are donated funds audited and tracked?

We maintain detailed financial ledgers and publish regular photo-documented field reports on our open archive portal for donor oversight.

Does the organization accept non-monetary donations?

Yes, we accept physical shipments of educational materials, clothing, and medical supplies. Please coordinate with Brother Irfan for shipping logistics.
