About Us

Hope Giver Foundation & Outreach Ministries is a faith-based organization committed to bringing hope, dignity, and practical support to deserving and underserved communities across Pakistan.

Our mission is to serve people with love, compassion and faith, while creating opportunities for children, youth, women and families to grow spiritually, educationally and economically. Through our different programs, we share the love of Jesus Christ, provide access to education, empower women with practical skills, support families in need and reach communities that are often overlooked

Frequently Asked Questions

WHAT WE BELIEVE?

We believe in Jesus Christ, His love, and His call to serve others. We believe faith should be lived through love, compassion, and action. “Let us not love with words or speech but with actions and in truth.” — 1 John 3:18

WHY WE SERVE?

We serve to bring hope, dignity, and opportunity to those facing difficult circumstances. Through education, empowerment, evangelism, and practical support, we walk alongside children, families, and communities—helping create a brighter future with lasting impact.

OUR IMPACT?

Through our ongoing ministries, we are creating meaningful opportunities and positive change for children, families, women, and vulnerable communities. Our impact is reflected in the lives we reach through education, empowerment, spiritual care, and practical community support.
